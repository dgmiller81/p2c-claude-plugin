# The Essential Stack for Discovery & Validation

If I had to keep only what actually moves the needle, here's the shortlist and the order to run it.

## The "Best of" Shortlist
- **Jobs-to-be-Done** — the single most useful lens for framing the problem
- **Mom Test interviews** — the only research method that consistently avoids false positives
- **Lean Canvas** — forces problem/solution/customer fit onto one page
- **Smoke-test landing page** — cheapest real demand signal you can get
- **RICE or ICE scoring** — for deciding what's worth building
- **Pre-defined kill criteria** — the discipline that prevents zombie projects

Everything else is supporting cast.

## How to Structure the Process

### Phase 1: Frame (Days 1–3)
- Write a **JTBD statement**: *"When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."*
- Draft a **Lean Canvas** — fill what you know, mark what you're guessing
- List your **top 3 riskiest assumptions** (usually: problem real? users will pay? you can reach them?)

### Phase 2: Talk to Humans (Weeks 1–2)
- Run **8–12 Mom Test interviews** with target users
- Rules: ask about their life, not your idea; dig into past behavior; listen for emotion + spend + workarounds
- After each batch of 3–5, update the Lean Canvas

**Signal you're looking for:** people describe the pain unprompted, have tried to solve it, and are spending time/money on workarounds.

### Phase 3: Test Demand (Weeks 2–3)
- Build a **smoke-test landing page** (Carrd or Framer, half a day's work)
- Drive 200–500 targeted visitors (Reddit, LinkedIn, niche communities, small ad spend)
- Measure: visit → email signup conversion (>5% is meaningful, >10% is strong)
- Optional: add a "reserve your spot" or pre-order button for stronger signal

### Phase 4: Decide (Day ~21)
- Score the opportunity with **RICE** (or ICE if early)
- Compare results against **pre-written kill criteria**, e.g.:
  - "Kill if <5 of 10 interviewees describe the pain unprompted"
  - "Kill if landing page converts <3%"
  - "Kill if no one offers to pay / pre-order"
- Write a **1-page go/no-go memo**

## The Minimum Viable Toolset
- **Notion or Google Docs** — interview notes, canvas, memo
- **Carrd or Framer** — landing page in hours, not days
- **Plausible or PostHog** — lightweight analytics on the page
- **Typeform or Tally** — short follow-up survey
- **Calendly** — book interviews without ping-pong

That's it. Six tools, four phases, ~3 weeks, one decision.

## The Mental Model
Discovery isn't research for research's sake — it's **systematically destroying your own assumptions** before they get expensive. The shortlist above is optimized for that: each step is designed to expose the lie in your idea as cheaply as possible.

If you survive all four phases, you've earned the right to build.
